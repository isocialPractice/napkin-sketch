    Direction
    Centre of rotation

    A preset puts the centre on the selection's box. Clicking the canvas puts it anywhere - on a shoulder, a heel, or off the shape entirely - and dragging from anywhere else turns the selection about it.

    Positive degrees turn clockwise, negative counterclockwise. Drag on the canvas to turn the selection by hand, or drag the crosshair to move the centre. Arrow keys step the angle; hold Shift for 15°, and for a snapped drag. Enter rotates again from where the selection now is; Escape closes.
