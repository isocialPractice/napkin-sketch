# Quickstart: transforming

Move, scale, turn, mirror, bend and combine what you drew: the **Transform**
menu in one sitting. Each change is one undo step, so `Ctrl + Z` takes any of
them back whole. [Transform, Rotate, Mirror, Mesh Warp, Liquify and Wipe Stacks](../guide/transform.md)
has the full detail of each.

## 1. Select something

1. `S` - the Select tool. Click a stroke, or drag a box round several.
2. `Shift`-click adds a stroke to the selection or takes it out; `Ctrl + A`
   selects everything on the page.

## 2. Move it

1. Drag the selection. Hold `Shift` while dragging to pin the movement to
   one axis.
2. `Enter` opens **Move** to type an exact distance, in any unit, with a live
   preview; the **Move** button applies it.

## 3. Scale it

1. `Ctrl + T`, the **Transform** button beside **Mirror** in the toolbar, or
   **Transform > Transform Box** puts the **Transform box** round the
   selection; the button stays pressed while the box is up.
2. Drag a corner to scale both ways, or a side to scale one. Hold `Shift` to
   keep the shape, `Alt` to scale from the centre.
3. `Ctrl + T` again, or `Escape`, puts the box away.

## 4. Turn it

1. `Ctrl + R` opens **Rotate**.
2. Type an angle and press `Enter`; the panel stays up for the next turn.
3. Or drag on the canvas while the panel is open to turn the selection by
   hand. `Escape` closes the panel.

## 5. Mirror it

1. `O` opens **Mirror**.
2. Pick **Horizontal**, **Vertical** or both, and tick **Create Copy** to leave
   the original where it is.
3. Press **Mirror**, or `Enter`.

## 6. Bend it

1. Pick **Mesh Warp** from below the tools in the side rail, and click the art
   to put a mesh of pins on it.
2. Drag a pin to bend the art round the others.
3. `Enter` keeps the bend as one undo step; `Escape` throws it away.
4. Or `Shift + R` - **Liquify** - and pick **Warp**, **Twirl**, **Pucker** or
   **Bloat** from its panel. Drag over the marks, or hold still on them, to
   bend what is under the brush; `[` and `]`, or an `Alt`-drag, size it. One
   drag is one undo step, and `Escape` before letting go puts it back.

## 7. Join, close and split strokes

1. Select two strokes and press `Ctrl + J` to **Join** them end to end into
   one stroke, on the first stroke's layer.
2. **Close Shape** in the toolbar joins a selected stroke's two ends: **Sharp**
   with a straight line, **Smooth** with a curve that carries the drawn
   directions through the seam.
3. `J` - **Split**, the other way: click a path to cut it there. A closed
   path opens at the first click, and a second divides it.

## 8. Combine shapes

1. Select two or more overlapping shapes.
2. **Transform > Wipe Stacks** - or right-click the canvas - and pick a row:
   **Wipe In** unites them, **Wipe Out** takes the top from below or below
   from the top, **Mid Wipe** keeps their overlap, **Outer Wipes** keeps all
   but it, and **Clean Wipe** cuts every piece apart.
3. A napkin wipes over the result for a quarter of a second; `Ctrl + Z`
   takes the whole wipe back.
