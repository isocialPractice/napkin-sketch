Pan & Zoom

Hold Alt and scroll the mouse wheel to zoom. By default scroll up zooms in and scroll down zooms out.

Scroll the wheel to pan vertically; hold Ctrl + Shift to pan horizontally.

Quick Features

Time allowed to type a value after pressing W (width) or Q (opacity).

Hold Shift while drawing to snap the stroke to the nearest endpoint of an existing stroke within the sensitivity radius.

When on, a stroke that snaps onto another stroke's endpoint (same tool and color) merges with it into one stroke.

Copic Marker

Hold the hold key for the hold time to show the nib indicator, then hold a rotate key to turn the broad nib. While active, the stroke width is scaled by the width multiplier (restored on release). The three keys must differ; a clashing pick is reassigned automatically.
