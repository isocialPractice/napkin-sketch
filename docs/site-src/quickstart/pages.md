# Quickstart: pages

A sketch book holds as many pages as you like. Add them three ways, size
them, move between them, and export them all at once. [Pages](../guide/pages.md)
has the full detail.

## 1. Show the pages panel

`Ctrl + B` shows or hides the thumbnail panel down the side; drag its edge to
make the thumbnails bigger.

## 2. Add a page like the one in view

Press **+ Page** at the top of the panel, or choose **Pages > Add Page >
Default New Page**: a blank page the same size as the one in view, turned to
with a page-turn.

## 3. Add a page of a size you type

1. Choose **Pages > Add Page > Custom New Page…** (the same row is in the
   panel's hamburger menu, beside **+ Page**).
2. Page Settings opens with **Sized page** already chosen. Type the width and
   height.
3. Press **Add Page**. Closing the dialog instead adds nothing.

## 4. Add a page from a selection

1. Select something on the page (`S`, then drag round it).
2. Choose **Pages > Add Page > From Selection**: the new page is the
   selection's size, and a copy of the selection comes with it, at its corner
   and selected. The originals stay where they were.

## 5. Size a page

Right-click the panel, or choose **Pages > Page Settings…**: an **endless**
page fills the window, which is how a new sketch starts; a **sized** page
keeps an exact width and height and shows a dashed outline.

## 6. Move between pages

Click a thumbnail to turn to its page; everything you drew stays where you
left it. `PageUp` and `PageDown` turn back and on a page, as do **Pages >
Previous Page** and **Next Page** and the **‹** and **›** at the right of the
status bar. **Pages > Delete Page** removes the page in view, and is greyed
while there is only one.

## 7. Export every page

Under **File > Export**, each format offers **Export all pages**: PNG, JPEG
and SVG write `name_1`, `name_2` and on, one file a page, and PDF writes every
page into one document.
